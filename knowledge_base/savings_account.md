# Savings Account (High-Yield / Premium)

## What it is
A Premium Savings Account is an upgrade to a standard savings account that
offers higher interest rates, no minimum balance penalties, priority banking,
and bundled benefits. It is designed for customers who maintain consistently
high account balances and prefer liquidity over locked-in returns.

## Who it is for
Premium Savings Accounts are ideal for customers who:
- Already maintain a savings account and want better returns on idle cash
- Prefer full liquidity (no lock-in period, unlike FD)
- Have a moderate-to-high average monthly balance (above ₹1,00,000)
- Receive regular salary or freelance credits and keep a healthy buffer
- Want banking relationship benefits without committing funds long-term

## Key Benefits
- Higher interest rates compared to standard savings accounts (up to 7% p.a.)
- No minimum balance penalties (for premium tier accounts)
- Priority customer service and dedicated relationship manager
- Free NEFT, RTGS, IMPS transactions
- Waiver on annual credit card fees
- Complimentary demat account or locker facility
- Interest calculated on daily balance (not monthly)

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹75,000
- Savings rate above 20%
- Expense-to-income ratio below 70%
- Average monthly balance above ₹1,00,000
- Liquidity-first preference (does not want to lock funds in FD)
- Multiple accounts or banking relationships to consolidate

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Interest Rate: 4% to 7% per annum (tiered by balance slab)
- Minimum Balance: ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 (varies by bank and tier)
- Free Transactions: Unlimited NEFT/IMPS, 5 free ATM withdrawals per month
- Additional Benefit: Relationship Manager assigned above ₹5L balance
