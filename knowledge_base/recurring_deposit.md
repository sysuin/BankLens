# Recurring Deposit (RD)

## What it is
A Recurring Deposit is a savings product where the customer deposits a fixed
amount every month for a predetermined tenure. At the end of the tenure, the
customer receives the total principal plus accumulated interest. It is designed
to cultivate a disciplined monthly savings habit.

## Who it is for
Recurring Deposits are ideal for customers who:
- Have a regular monthly income (salary) and want to save a fixed amount each month
- Are building toward a specific financial goal (vacation, appliance, wedding)
- Cannot afford to lock a large lump sum in an FD but want similar returns
- Show consistent monthly spending discipline with a positive savings balance
- Are in the early-to-mid stage of their savings journey

## Key Benefits
- Builds a savings habit automatically with monthly auto-debit
- Guaranteed returns — interest rate fixed at the time of account opening
- Flexible tenures: 6 months to 10 years
- Loan against RD available (up to 90% of deposited amount)
- Premature closure allowed (with a small penalty)
- No market risk — fully protected principal

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income between ₹25,000 and ₹1,00,000
- Savings rate between 10% and 30%
- Expense-to-income ratio between 65% and 85%
- Regular salary credits visible in the bank statement
- Goal-oriented saver working toward a 1–5 year financial target
- Low-to-medium risk appetite

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Monthly Installment: ₹500 to ₹1,00,000 per month
- Tenure: 6 months to 10 years
- Interest Rate: 6% to 7.5% per annum (compounded quarterly)
- Minimum Installment: ₹100 per month (varies by bank)
- Penalty for missed installment: ₹1.5 per ₹100 per month
- TDS applicable on interest above ₹40,000 per year
