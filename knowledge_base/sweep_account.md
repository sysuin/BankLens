# Sweep-In Fixed Deposit (Sweep Account)

## What it is
A Sweep-In Fixed Deposit is a hybrid product that links a savings account to
a fixed deposit. When the savings account balance exceeds a set threshold,
the surplus amount is automatically "swept" into an FD and earns FD-level
interest. When the savings account needs funds, the FD is broken in reverse
chronological order (LIFO) to cover the shortfall — without any manual action.

## Who it is for
Sweep accounts are ideal for customers who:
- Maintain a high average balance in their savings account (above ₹50,000)
- Want FD-level returns without sacrificing liquidity
- Have variable monthly expenses and do not want to lock all their savings
- Show a pattern of large, irregular transfers to savings in their statement
- Are HNI (High Net Worth Individual) customers or senior professionals

## Key Benefits
- Earns FD interest on idle savings account balance automatically
- Full liquidity maintained — funds accessible at any time
- No need to manually open or close FDs
- Higher returns than a standard savings account (1%–3% more)
- Ideal for parking emergency funds that still need to earn returns
- Tax benefit under Section 80C if the FD tenure is 5 years

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹1,00,000
- Savings rate above 30% with a large idle savings balance
- Expense-to-income ratio below 60%
- Liquidity-conscious — does not want to commit to a fixed tenure
- High balance volatility: some months spend more, some months save more
- Prefers automated financial management over manual fund transfers

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Minimum Balance for Sweep: ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 (varies by bank)
- Sweep Threshold: Configurable (e.g. any balance above ₹50,000 sweeps to FD)
- FD Interest Rate: 6.5% to 7.5% per annum (same as regular FD)
- Savings Rate on Balance Below Threshold: 3%–4% per annum
- Minimum Sweep Amount: ₹5,000 per sweep
- Breakage: LIFO (last-in, first-out) — most recent FD broken first
