# Debt Consolidation Loan (Balance Transfer & EMI Reduction)

## What it is
A Debt Consolidation Loan is a specialized personal loan designed to merge
multiple high-cost debts or cover cashflow deficits into a single, lower-interest
monthly EMI. It helps customers undergoing temporary financial stress reduce their
overall debt servicing obligations and prevent default risk.

## Who it is for
Debt Consolidation Loans are specifically tailored for customers who:
- Face cashflow stress with high expense-to-income ratios (>85%) or negative savings rates
- Spend a significant portion of income on medical emergencies, rent, or recurring debts
- Have a stable monthly salary credit but suffer from high immediate outlays
- Risk defaulting on credit cards or high-interest informal loans if outlays are not restructured
- Need lower monthly payments and an extended repayment tenure to restore financial stability

## Key Benefits
- Reduced monthly outflow: lowers immediate EMI burden by extending tenure or reducing interest rates
- Prevents default: protects customer's credit score before missed payments occur
- Single predictable EMI: replaces multiple fragmented debt payments
- Structured repayment plan: 24 to 60 months tenure for manageable budgeting
- Financial relief: restores net cashflow to positive territory

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income: ₹25,000 to ₹75,000
- Expense-to-income ratio: >85% (High financial stress / cashflow pressure)
- Savings rate: Low (<10%) or Negative (Cashflow Deficit)
- Stable employment/salary visible in statement
- High risk profile — requires risk-aware underwriting with debt relief focus

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Loan Amount: ₹50,000 to ₹15,00,000
- Interest Rate: 11% to 15% per annum (lower than credit card revolving rates)
- Tenure: 24 to 60 months
- End-use: Debt restructuring and cashflow stabilization
