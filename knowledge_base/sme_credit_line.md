# SME Business Overdraft & Working Capital Credit Line

## What it is
An SME Business Credit Line / Overdraft is a revolving credit facility granted to self-employed professionals, freelancers, and small business owners to manage seasonal cashflow gaps, inventory purchasing, and operational outlays.

## Who it is for
SME Credit Lines are ideal for customers who:
- Run a small business, consultancy, or freelance practice
- Experience fluctuating monthly inflows or client payment delays
- Require flexible liquidity without paying interest on unutilized limits
- Need short-term working capital financing

## Key Benefits
- Pay interest ONLY on the exact amount withdrawn and utilized, not the full sanctioned limit
- Revolving limit: repay and reuse funds as receivables arrive
- No pre-payment penalties for early settlement
- Instant digital withdrawal via mobile banking
- Collateral-free options available up to ₹25 Lakhs for eligible businesses

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Business turnover or monthly credits > ₹1,000,000
- Fluctuating month-on-month credit inflow
- Medium credit risk rating
- Active commercial current/savings account history

## Indicative Terms
- Credit Limit: ₹2 Lakhs to ₹50 Lakhs
- Interest Rate: 10.5% – 14.0% p.a. (charged on daily utilization)
- Facility Renewal: Annual review
- Processing Fee: 0.50% to 1.00% of sanctioned limit
