# Personal Loan

## What it is
A Personal Loan is an unsecured loan offered to individuals for personal
financial needs such as medical emergencies, travel, weddings, home renovation,
or debt consolidation. No collateral is required. The loan amount is disbursed
as a lump sum and repaid in fixed monthly EMIs over the loan tenure.

## Who it is for
Personal Loans are suitable for customers who:
- Have high expense-to-income ratios indicating financial stress or large one-time needs
- Show irregular or elevated spending in categories like Health, Shopping, or Travel
- Have a stable monthly income (salary or freelance) to service EMI payments
- Need immediate liquidity without pledging assets
- Have a healthy credit history with consistent income credits in their statement

## Key Benefits
- No collateral required — purely income-based eligibility
- Quick disbursal: often within 24–48 hours
- Flexible loan amounts: ₹50,000 to ₹40,00,000
- Flexible tenures: 12 to 60 months
- Fixed EMIs make budgeting predictable
- Can be used for any purpose — no end-use restriction

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹25,000
- Steady salary or freelance credits visible in the bank statement
- Expense-to-income ratio between 70%–90% (some financial pressure)
- Low-to-medium existing debt obligations
- Clear income pattern over at least 3 months

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Loan Amount: ₹50,000 to ₹40,00,000
- Interest Rate: 10.5% to 18% per annum (depends on credit score and income)
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months
- Processing Fee: 1%–3% of loan amount
- Prepayment allowed after 12 EMIs (with penalty)
