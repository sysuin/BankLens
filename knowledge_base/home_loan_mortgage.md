# Home Loan & Mortgage

## What it is
A Home Loan is a secured long-term credit facility provided by the bank to finance the purchase, construction, or renovation of residential property. The property serves as collateral until the loan is fully repaid.

## Who it is for
Home Loans are ideal for customers who:
- Have a stable, consistent monthly salary or business income
- Maintain a disciplined savings rate (above 30% of net income)
- Have a low or medium expense-to-income ratio (< 60%)
- Are planning to purchase a primary residence or real estate investment
- Wish to leverage tax benefits under section 80C and 24(b)

## Key Benefits
- Competitive interest rates starting from 8.35% to 9.25% p.a.
- Flexible tenures ranging up to 30 years for lower monthly EMIs
- Tax savings on interest payments (up to ₹2 Lakhs under Sec 24b) and principal repayment (up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Sec 80C)
- Balance transfer option to switch existing higher-interest home loans
- Top-up loan facility available for existing home loan borrowers

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹75,000
- Expense-to-income ratio below 65%
- Stable credit score (CIBIL > 750)
- Low credit default risk rating

## Indicative Terms
- Interest Rate: 8.35% – 9.15% per annum (floating rate linked to EBLR)
- Tenure: 5 years to 30 years
- Processing Fee: 0.25% to 0.50% of loan amount
- Max Loan-to-Value (LTV): 80% – 90% of property valuation
