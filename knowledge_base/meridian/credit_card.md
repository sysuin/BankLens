# Credit Card

## What it is
A Credit Card is a revolving credit facility that allows customers to make
purchases and payments up to a defined credit limit. The outstanding balance
can be repaid in full each month (interest-free) or carried forward with
interest charges. Credit cards also offer rewards, cashback, and milestone benefits.

## Who it is for
Credit Cards are well-suited for customers who:
- Have high monthly spending across categories like Food, Shopping, Subscriptions,
  and Transport
- Pay their bills in full and on time (disciplined spenders)
- Spend frequently on e-commerce or lifestyle merchants (Amazon, Zomato, Netflix)
- Travel frequently and spend on airlines, hotels, or travel apps
- Want to earn rewards, cashback, or air miles on everyday spending

## Key Benefits
- Interest-free credit period of up to 50 days on purchases
- Reward points on every transaction (redeemable for vouchers, flight miles, cashback)
- Cashback on specific merchant categories (Food, Fuel, Grocery)
- EMI conversion for large purchases
- Complimentary airport lounge access (premium cards)
- Zero liability on fraudulent transactions
- Credit score building with responsible usage

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹30,000
- Regular spending across multiple merchant categories
- Expense-to-income ratio between 50%–85% (active spender)
- Consistent monthly salary or income credits visible in the statement
- At least 3–6 months of banking history

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Credit Limit: ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000 (based on income and credit score)
- Annual Fee: ₹500 to ₹5,000 (waived on meeting annual spend targets)
- Interest Rate: 36%–42% per annum on revolving balance (if not paid in full)
- Minimum Payment Due: 5% of outstanding balance per month
- Joining Bonus: Welcome rewards worth ₹500–₹2,000 depending on card variant
