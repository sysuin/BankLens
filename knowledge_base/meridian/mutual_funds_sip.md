# Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) & Wealth Mutual Funds

## What it is
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows customers to invest a fixed sum automatically every month into professional equity, hybrid, or debt mutual funds. It harnesses rupee-cost averaging and compounding to build long-term wealth.

## Who it is for
SIP Mutual Funds are ideal for customers who:
- Have regular monthly surplus income (savings rate > 20%)
- Seek higher long-term wealth growth (12%–15% historical equity CAGR) compared to traditional savings accounts
- Prefer disciplined, automated monthly investing over lump-sum market timing
- Are planning long-term financial goals (retirement, house down payment, child education)

## Key Benefits
- Automated monthly debit starting as low as ₹500/month
- Rupee-cost averaging reduces impact of short-term stock market volatility
- Power of compounding returns over 3 to 10+ year horizons
- High liquidity: redeem funds anytime with 1-day settlement (for open-ended funds)
- Tax-saving ELSS funds provide tax deduction under Sec 80C

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹40,000
- Savings rate above 20%
- Medium to high risk tolerance for equity funds; low risk for debt/liquid funds
- Consistent positive net cashflow

## Indicative Terms
- Minimum SIP Amount: ₹500 to ₹1,000 per month
- Expected Returns: 11% – 14% p.a. (historical equity average, market linked)
- Lock-in: No lock-in (except ELSS funds which have a 3-year lock-in)
- Exit Load: Nil after 1 year of holding
