# Harbor Term Deposit

## Overview
A fixed-rate, fixed-term deposit for money a member will not need for 6 to 60 months. The rate is locked at opening, so the return is known on day one.

## Interest Rates (per annum)
- 6 months: 4.00%
- 12 months: 4.40%
- 24 months: 4.55%
- 36 to 60 months: 4.70%
- Members over 60 receive an additional 0.25%

## Key Features
- Minimum deposit 1,000; maximum 500,000 per term
- Interest paid monthly, annually or at maturity
- Automatic renewal with a 10-day grace period to change terms
- Early redemption allowed after 90 days at a reduced rate, never a loss of principal
- Can be pledged as security for a Harbor loan at a preferential rate

## Eligibility
- Harbor member with a funded savings or chequing account

## Ideal For
Members with a consistently high savings rate and a cash buffer already in place, who want a guaranteed return on the surplus without market exposure.

## Cross-Sell Notes
Position after the High-Yield Savings Account is established. For members with very high surplus, ladder terms (6, 12, 24 months) to keep liquidity.
