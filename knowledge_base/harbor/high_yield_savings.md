# Harbor High-Yield Savings Account

## Overview
A no-lock-in savings account paying a tiered rate that rises with the balance. Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly, and funds can be moved to the Everyday Chequing Account instantly.

## Interest Rates
- 0 to 25,000: 3.10% per annum
- 25,000 to 100,000: 3.60% per annum
- Above 100,000: 3.85% per annum
- Introductory bonus of 0.50% for the first 6 months on new money

## Key Features
- No monthly fee, no minimum balance
- Instant transfers to and from any Harbor account
- Automatic "pay yourself first" transfer on payday
- Savings goals with named buckets (emergency fund, holiday, tax)

## Eligibility
- Harbor member with any chequing account
- Available as a joint or trust account

## Ideal For
Members with a positive savings rate who are holding surplus cash in a chequing account. The right first home for an emergency fund before any term product is discussed.

## Cross-Sell Notes
Lead with this for any member with a savings rate above 15% and a rising balance. Once the emergency fund reaches three months of expenses, introduce the Term Deposit for the excess.
