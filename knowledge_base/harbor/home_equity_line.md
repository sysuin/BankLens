# Harbor Home Equity Line of Credit

## Overview
A revolving line of credit secured against the member's home, for renovations, education or consolidating higher-interest debt at a lower rate.

## Rates and Terms
- Variable rate: prime plus 0.50% (currently 7.45% per annum)
- Borrow up to 65% of the appraised home value, less any existing mortgage
- Interest-only minimum payments; repay principal any time without penalty
- Appraisal fee 300, waived for lines above 100,000

## Eligibility
- Harbor member who owns the property with at least 35% equity
- Verified income sufficient to service the line at a stress-tested rate of prime plus 2%
- Not in cash-flow deficit on the most recent three months of statements

## Ideal For
Homeowning members with a stable income who want low-cost, flexible borrowing capacity, or who are paying more than 15% on existing unsecured debt.

## Cross-Sell Notes
A consolidation conversation for members carrying card debt with a Medium risk band and home ownership signals (mortgage payments or property tax in the statement). Never position as spending money for members already in deficit.
