# Harbor Auto Loan

## Overview
A fixed-rate loan for new or used vehicles, with the vehicle as security and no prepayment penalty.

## Rates and Terms
- New vehicles: from 6.49% per annum, up to 84 months
- Used vehicles (under 7 years): from 7.49% per annum, up to 60 months
- Loan amounts 5,000 to 120,000
- No prepayment penalty; skip-a-payment once per year in good standing

## Eligibility
- Harbor member with verified monthly income of at least 2,500
- Total debt payments including the new loan below 40% of gross monthly income
- Positive cash flow on the most recent statement

## Ideal For
Members with steady income and a moderate savings rate who show transport spending (fuel, ride-hailing, public transit) and would benefit from owning a vehicle at a fixed cost.

## Cross-Sell Notes
Look for recurring transport and ride-hailing spend with a Low or Medium risk band. Pair with the Everyday Chequing Account for automatic payments and a rate discount of 0.25%.
