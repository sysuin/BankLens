# Harbor Index Investment Plan

## Overview
A low-cost, automatic monthly investment into diversified index funds, managed through Harbor's wealth partner. Designed for members building long-term wealth after their emergency fund is in place.

## Key Features
- Start from 100 per month; change or pause any time
- Portfolios from conservative (30% equity) to growth (90% equity)
- Total annual cost 0.45% including fund fees
- Automatic rebalancing and annual tax statements
- Tax-advantaged retirement and education wrappers available

## Eligibility
- Harbor member aged 18 or older
- A funded High-Yield Savings Account holding at least one month of expenses
- Investment horizon of five years or more

## Ideal For
Members with a high savings rate, an established cash buffer and no high-interest debt, who want growth beyond deposit rates and can leave the money invested through market cycles.

## Cross-Sell Notes
The final step in the savings ladder: chequing, then high-yield savings, then term deposits, then this plan. Recommend only for Low risk band members with a savings rate above 25%.
