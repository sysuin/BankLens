# Harbor Cash-Back Credit Card

## Overview
A no-annual-fee credit card that returns a share of everyday spending as cash credited to the member's chequing account each quarter.

## Rewards
- 3% cash back on groceries and fuel
- 1.5% cash back on everything else
- No cap on rewards; no annual fee
- 21 days interest-free on purchases when the balance is paid in full

## Rates and Fees
- Purchase interest rate: 19.99% per annum
- Cash advance rate: 22.99% per annum
- Foreign transaction fee: 2.5%
- Late payment fee: 25

## Eligibility
- Harbor member aged 18 or older
- Verified monthly income of at least 1,800
- Positive cash flow: monthly income must exceed monthly expenses on the most recent statement
- Credit limit set at up to one month of verified income, reviewed annually

## Ideal For
Members with a stable surplus who already spend on groceries and fuel and pay balances in full. It is a rewards product, not a borrowing product.

## Cross-Sell Notes
Recommend only to members with a positive savings rate and a Low or Medium risk band. Do not recommend to members in cash-flow deficit or with a High risk band; for them, discuss the Everyday Chequing overdraft cushion and a savings plan instead.
