# Harbor Small Business Line of Credit

## Overview
An unsecured revolving line for sole traders and small companies to smooth cash flow between invoices and payroll.

## Rates and Terms
- Limits from 10,000 to 150,000
- Variable rate: prime plus 2.50% (currently 9.45% per annum)
- Pay interest only on what is drawn; annual review fee 150
- Draw and repay through online banking with same-day availability

## Eligibility
- Business member of Harbor with 12 months of trading history
- Business statements showing regular incoming payments from customers
- Owner's personal and business accounts not in sustained deficit
- Personal guarantee from the owner

## Ideal For
Members whose statements show business income patterns (multiple customer payments, supplier payments, payroll) and short cash gaps between receivables and outgoings.

## Cross-Sell Notes
Trigger on business-like inflows and outflows. Not for members with a High risk band; for them, discuss invoice timing and a savings buffer first.
