# Harbor Everyday Chequing Account

## Overview
The Harbor Everyday Chequing Account is the credit union's core transaction account for members who want no-surprise banking: unlimited debit transactions, free e-transfers, and no monthly fee when a payroll deposit lands each month.

## Key Features
- No monthly fee with a recurring payroll or pension deposit of at least 1,500 per month; otherwise 6.00 per month
- Unlimited debit purchases, bill payments and e-transfers
- Two free non-Harbor ATM withdrawals per month
- Overdraft cushion of 250 with no interest for the first 3 days
- Round-up savings: every purchase rounded to the nearest 10 and swept to a Harbor savings account

## Eligibility
- Member of Harbor Credit Union (one-time 5 membership share)
- Age 18 or older, or a joint account with a guardian
- No minimum balance

## Ideal For
Members whose income arrives regularly and who want a simple, fee-free primary account with automatic savings on top. A natural pairing for salaried customers with a moderate savings rate who have not yet separated spending from saving.

## Cross-Sell Notes
Position first for members with steady monthly credits and a healthy but unstructured savings pattern. Combine with the High-Yield Savings Account via the round-up feature.
