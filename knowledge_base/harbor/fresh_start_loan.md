# Harbor Fresh Start Consolidation Loan

## Overview
A fixed-rate, fixed-term personal loan for members whose monthly outgoings have overtaken their income. It pays off higher-interest balances (cards, overdraft, payday advances) in one transaction and replaces them with a single lower monthly payment, so the member's cashflow returns to positive and default is avoided.

## Who it is for
Members who:
- Are in cashflow deficit: expenses above income, a negative savings rate, or an expense-to-income ratio above 85%
- Carry revolving balances on cards or an overdraft that are growing month on month
- Have a stable payroll or pension deposit visible in their statement
- Are rated high credit risk today and need their monthly obligations lowered, not another line of credit

## Rates and Terms
- Fixed rate from 9.90% per annum (well below card and overdraft rates)
- Amounts from 2,000 to 40,000; term 12 to 60 months
- Payment date aligned to payday; no prepayment penalty
- Optional rate reduction of 0.25% after 12 consecutive on-time payments

## Key Features
- One payment, one date, one rate: replaces several fragmented minimum payments
- Balances are paid directly to the other lenders at funding
- Card and overdraft limits are reduced or closed as a condition of the loan, so the debt does not rebuild
- Free budgeting session with a member advisor at funding and at month six

## Eligibility
- Harbor member with a payroll or pension deposit of at least 1,500 per month
- Not intended as new spending money; funds go to existing creditors

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Savings rate negative or below 5%
- Expense-to-income ratio above 85%; high credit risk band
- Regular salary credit with rent, utilities and debt repayments dominating spending

## Cross-Sell Notes
This is the only borrowing product to lead with for a member in deficit. Never pair it with the Cashback Credit Card or a new line of credit; pair it with the Everyday Chequing Account's round-up savings so a buffer rebuilds as the balance falls.
