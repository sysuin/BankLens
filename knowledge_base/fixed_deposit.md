# Fixed Deposit (FD)

## What it is
A Fixed Deposit is a savings instrument where the customer deposits a lump sum
amount with the bank for a fixed tenure at a predetermined interest rate.
The principal and interest are guaranteed regardless of market conditions,
making it one of the safest investment options available.

## Who it is for
Fixed Deposits are ideal for customers who:
- Have a consistent savings habit and regularly transfer money to a savings account
- Maintain a high savings rate (above 25% of monthly income)
- Prefer capital safety over high returns
- Have surplus idle cash sitting in a current or savings account
- Are building an emergency fund or saving toward a specific goal

## Key Benefits
- Guaranteed returns: interest rate is locked at the time of opening
- Flexible tenures: 7 days to 10 years
- Premature withdrawal available (with a small penalty)
- Auto-renewal option available
- Senior citizen benefit: additional 0.25%–0.50% interest rate
- Loans against FD available at low interest rates

## Ideal Customer Profile
- Monthly income above ₹50,000
- Expense-to-income ratio below 70%
- Savings rate above 25%
- Low to medium risk appetite
- No immediate liquidity requirements for the locked amount

## Indicative Terms (illustrative, not actual rates)
- Tenure: 1 year to 5 years
- Interest Rate: 6.5% to 7.5% per annum (varies by bank and tenure)
- Minimum Deposit: ₹5,000 to ₹10,000
- TDS applicable on interest above ₹40,000 per year
